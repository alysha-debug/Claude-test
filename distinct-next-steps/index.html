Step 3

Your document checklist.

Here's what each document looks like and where to find it. Upload everything to your TaxDome portal.

0 of 3 ready

  • [IMAGE: PRIOR TAX RETURN MOCK-UP]
    1

    Prior tax returns

    Where to find it: your copy from whoever prepared your return, or the PDF saved in your tax software. Include personal and business returns.

  • [IMAGE: YEAR-TO-DATE PROFIT AND LOSS MOCK-UP]
    2

    Year-to-date profit and loss

    Where to find it: run a Profit and Loss report in your bookkeeping software, dated from January 1 to today, or ask your bookkeeper for it.

  • [IMAGE: IRS OR STATE LETTER MOCK-UP]
    3

    Any IRS or state letters

    Where to find it: notices mailed to you by the IRS or your state tax agency. The notice number is usually printed in the top right corner.

Partial uploads are fine. Upload what you have now and add the rest as you find it.

Books behind? We're a judgment-free firm. Upload what you have and note what's missing in the organizer.
